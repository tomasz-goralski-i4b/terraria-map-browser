## Reference material

- [Official Wiki: The Underworld](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Hell): broad open space, ash terrain, flat lava pools and rectangular obsidian/hellstone ruined houses.
- [Official Wiki: Cavern](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Cavern): stone terrain, ores and ordinary cave features.
- [Underworld screenshot](https://pinion.jp/%E3%82%AC%E3%82%A4%E3%83%89/%E3%83%86%E3%83%A9%E3%83%AA%E3%82%A2-%E5%85%A8%E3%83%90%E3%82%A4%E3%82%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%A0%E8%A7%A3%E8%AA%AC/).

- Current right-side selection illustration: pixel scale, texture and color reference.

Downloaded game screenshots stay in ignored `.tdd/brand-references/`. Only original generated artwork is saved here; game sprites and screenshots are not shipped. All concepts have real alpha outside the world silhouette, while interiors retain their own shading. They exclude the earlier giant glowing geodes and ornate fiery arches.
